## Exact layout and tokens (measured from their site at 1440px)

- **Page:** white. A **left rail**: fixed/sticky, full height, 500px wide on a 1440 screen
  (scales: `width: calc(50vw - 220px)`, min 240px), with a 1px right border `rgba(11,16,21,.08)`.
  Rail content is right-aligned within the rail so it starts at x≈235: a block ~190px wide.
- **Main column:** starts 50px right of the rail border, `max-width: 640px`. A thin 1px vertical line
  also runs at the far right edge of the page (they have one at x≈1425); keep it.
- **Fonts:** Geist (body, headings) and Geist Mono (labels, buttons). next/font/google.
- **Colors:** ink `#0B1015`; secondary text `rgba(11,16,21,.6)` and `.68`; muted `.45`; hairlines
  `rgba(11,16,21,.14)`; surface `#F4F4F6`. **Our brand green `#0A7A4B` replaces their blue `#0562EF`
  everywhere they use blue** (links, active nav dot, primary button, highlighted bar, chart line).
  Light green tint `rgba(10,122,75,.07)` replaces their blue tint.
- **Type scale:** H1 40px/1.15, weight 400, letter-spacing -1.2px. H2 24px weight 400, -0.48px.
  "In production" statement 32px. Body 17px/27.2px. Card titles 15px weight 500. Small 13px/19.5px.
  Mono labels 11px weight 500, uppercase, letter-spacing .66px, color .6. Buttons: Geist Mono 12px
  weight 500, uppercase, letter-spacing ~.06em.
- **Shapes:** radius 1px (essentially square) on everything except avatars. Cards: 1px border
  `rgba(11,16,21,.14)`, and the hero media/primary cards get their shadow:
  `0 1px 2px rgba(11,16,21,.05), 0 12px 32px -12px rgba(11,16,21,.18), 0 32px 64px -24px rgba(11,16,21,.16)`.
- **Motion:** their `arrive` pattern: elements with `data-reveal` fade/rise in (opacity 0→1, 8px→0,
  ~600ms) when they enter the viewport (IntersectionObserver, rootMargin bottom -8%). The rail nav
  items stagger in on load; the logo strokes draw in. Charts animate when revealed (bars grow,
  heatmap cells turn on, line draws). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
